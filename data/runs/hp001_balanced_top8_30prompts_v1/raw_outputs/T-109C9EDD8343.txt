Here's an explanation of RAM that a beginner can understand:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while the computer is running. It's like a desk where your computer can quickly access the information it needs, without having to physically retrieve it from a hard drive or other storage device. Think of RAM as the "working memory" of your computer, where it can rapidly process information and perform tasks like browsing the internet, playing games, or working on documents. The more RAM your computer has, the more applications you can run at the same time without experiencing slower performance or freezing up.
