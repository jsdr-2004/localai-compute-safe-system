Here are three bullets summarizing the role of a privilege broker:

• **Mediation and negotiation**: Privilege brokers act as intermediaries between individuals or organizations with conflicting interests, facilitating negotiations to resolve disputes and find mutually beneficial solutions.

• **Identifying and addressing power imbalances**: By understanding the underlying power dynamics at play, privilege brokers can help identify areas where unequal relationships may be contributing to conflicts or issues. They then work to address these imbalances and create a more equitable environment for all parties involved.

• **Fostering inclusive decision-making processes**: Privilege brokers often facilitate open and honest discussions among diverse stakeholders, helping to ensure that everyone's voices are heard and their concerns are addressed in the decision-making process. This can lead to more informed, inclusive, and effective outcomes.
