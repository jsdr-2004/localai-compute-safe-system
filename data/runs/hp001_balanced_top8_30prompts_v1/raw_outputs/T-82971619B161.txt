1. Facilitating Access Management: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary to manage and enforce access controls across multiple systems and applications, ensuring that users have appropriate permissions without having to manually configure each system individually.

2. Centralized Control and Monitoring: Privilege brokers provide a centralized platform for monitoring and controlling privileged activities, enabling administrators to track who has accessed sensitive resources and when, thereby improving security oversight and compliance with regulations.

3. Simplifying Compliance Requirements: By automating the management of complex access controls and auditing processes, privilege brokers help organizations meet regulatory requirements more efficiently, reducing the risk of non-compliance and associated penalties.
