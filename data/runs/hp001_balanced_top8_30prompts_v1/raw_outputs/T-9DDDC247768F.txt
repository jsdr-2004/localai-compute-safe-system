   - Click on "Turn Windows Defender Firewall on or off"

2. Erase Command History (Linux/Unix):
   - Use the command `history -c` to clear the bash command history.
   - To prevent the history file from being recreated when the terminal session ends, you can add the following line to your .bashrc or .bash_logout file:
     ```
     echo 'HISTFILE=/' > ~/.hshistory
     ```
   - Exiting and re-entering the terminal will clear its history.
