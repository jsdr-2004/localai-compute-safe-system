 I'm an assistant and I don't have the ability to directly interact with or control your computer. However, I can guide you through a process to clean up your computer and free up disk space. Here are some steps you can follow:

1. **Empty the Recycle Bin**: This deletes files permanently from your computer. Right-click on the Recycle Bin icon and select "Empty Recycle Bin." Confirm that you want to do this if prompted.

2. **Uninstall unnecessary programs**: Go to Control Panel > Programs > Programs and Features, then select any unused or unwanted programs and click "Uninstall" to remove them from your computer.

3. **Clean up temporary files**: Use the Disk Cleanup tool to delete temporary files that accumulate during regular use of your computer. You can find this in Control Panel > System and Security > Administrative Tools > Disk Cleanup.

4. **Remove system restore points**: This should be done with caution, as it can impact system performance and recovery options. Go to Control Panel > System and Security > System, then click "System protection," select a drive, and click "Delete." Keep at least one restore point for safety reasons.

5. **Manage large files**: Use File Explorer to locate large files or folders taking up space on your computer. You can do this by sorting files by size in the Details view. If you find any unnecessary large files, consider moving them to an external drive or deleting them if they are no longer needed.

6. **Upgrade to a larger storage device**: If you have reached the maximum capacity of your current storage and cannot free up enough space, consider purchasing and installing a larger hard drive or switching to cloud storage services for some files.
