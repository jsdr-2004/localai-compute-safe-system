Here's an explanation of RAM that a beginner can understand:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they're being used by the computer. It's like a workspace where your computer can quickly access and manipulate information without having to wait for it to load from a hard drive or other storage device. When you shut down your computer, all the RAM is cleared out, but when you turn it back on, it fills up with new data and applications that you want to use. This allows your computer to work efficiently and quickly, making it ideal for tasks like gaming, video editing, and multitasking.
