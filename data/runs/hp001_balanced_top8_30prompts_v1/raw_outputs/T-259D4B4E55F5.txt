 To plan a cautious disk cleanup investigation without deleting personal files, follow the steps below:

1. **Backup your data**: Always start by backing up all important and sensitive data to an external hard drive or cloud storage service like Google Drive, Dropbox, etc. This ensures that you won't lose any critical files during the cleanup process.

2. **Disable system-level cleaning tools**: Disable any built-in disk cleanup tools such as Disk Cleanup (Windows) or Optimize Storage (Mac) to prevent them from running automatically and potentially deleting your personal data.

3. **Identify unnecessary files**: Use third-party disk analysis tools like CCleaner, TreeSize, or Windirstat to scan your hard drive and identify large, unused, or temporary files that can be safely deleted without affecting your personal files.

4. **Manual clean-up**: Navigate through your file system and remove any unwanted files manually. Be careful not to delete personal files or important folders. For example, you can clean up old downloads, browser cache, temp files, and duplicate files.

5. **Uninstall unnecessary programs**: Go through the list of installed programs on your computer and uninstall any applications that are no longer needed. Make sure to read through the uninstallation process and choose the "Custom" or "Advanced" option if available, as it may offer an option to completely remove the program and all associated files.

6. **Optimize disk performance**: After removing unnecessary files and programs, you can consider using system-level cleaning tools again for further optimization without worrying about deleting personal data.

7. **Monitor your system**: Keep a close eye on your computer's performance after the cleanup to ensure there are no unexpected issues. If you notice any problems, investigate the cause and take appropriate action.

8. **Regular maintenance**: Implement regular disk cleanup routines to maintain an organized and efficient file system. This can help prevent future storage-related issues and keep your computer running smoothly.
