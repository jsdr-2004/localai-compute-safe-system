For a 16GB CPU-only laptop, I would recommend the following models:

1. **Dell XPS 15**: The Dell XPS 15 is a popular and powerful laptop that offers a 3.5-inch display, Intel Core i7 processor, and up to 64GB of RAM. It's a great option for demanding tasks like video editing, 3D modeling, and gaming.
2. **Lenovo ThinkPad P53**: The Lenovo ThinkPad P53 is a high-performance laptop designed for business users who require intense processing power. It features an Intel Xeon processor, up to 128GB of RAM, and a massive 15.6-inch display. This model would be ideal for tasks that require extreme multitasking and resource-intensive applications.

Between these two options, I would choose the **Dell XPS 15** for several reasons:

* The Dell XPS 15 offers more flexibility in terms of display sizes and configurations, which can be beneficial if you need a larger screen or prefer a specific aspect ratio.
* The Intel Core i7 processor provides excellent performance for demanding tasks, and the laptop's cooling system is designed to keep it running smoothly even during intense usage.
* The Dell XPS 15 also offers more customization options, including the ability to upgrade the RAM and storage as needed.

In contrast, while the Lenovo ThinkPad P53 is an extremely powerful laptop, its massive size (15.6 inches) may make it less portable than the Dell XPS 15. Additionally, the ThinkPad P53's high-end features come at a premium price point, which may not be justified for most users.

Overall, the Dell XPS 15 offers a great balance of performance, portability, and customization options, making it an excellent choice for a 16GB CPU-only laptop.
