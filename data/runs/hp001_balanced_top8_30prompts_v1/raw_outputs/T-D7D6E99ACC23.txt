Here's an explanation of RAM for a beginner:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer storage that temporarily holds data and applications while your computer is running. Think of it like a workspace where you can quickly access the things you need without having to search through files or folders. When you open a program or save a document, it's stored in RAM so that your computer can use it more efficiently, but once you close it, it gets deleted from memory and can be reused by other programs. Having enough RAM is important because it affects how smoothly your computer runs, with too little RAM leading to slow performance and crashes.
