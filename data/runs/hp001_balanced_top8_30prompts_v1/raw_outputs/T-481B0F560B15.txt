1. Facilitating Access Management: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary to manage and enforce access controls across multiple systems and applications, ensuring that users have appropriate permissions based on their roles and responsibilities.

2. Enhancing Security Compliance: By centralizing and standardizing the management of user privileges, a privilege broker helps organizations meet regulatory requirements and security standards by providing clear audit trails and compliance reports.

3. Improving Operational Efficiency: Privilege brokers streamline access control processes through automation, reducing manual intervention and minimizing errors associated with managing permissions across different systems. This leads to faster onboarding and off-boarding of users while maintaining stringent security controls.
