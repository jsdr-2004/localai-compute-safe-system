 1. A privilege broker serves as an intermediary between different entities, facilitating access to privileged information or resources. This can include IT systems, data, or other assets that require high levels of security and authentication.

2. They are responsible for managing and enforcing access controls to ensure that only authorized individuals or systems can access these privileged resources. This often involves the use of tools like Identity and Access Management (IAM) solutions and role-based access control (RBAC).

3. Privilege brokers also monitor and log activities related to these privileged resources for auditing and compliance purposes, helping organizations maintain secure environments while enabling efficient workflows for their users.
