Here's an explanation of RAM for a beginner:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer storage that temporarily holds data and applications while your computer is running. Think of it like a workspace where you can quickly access and work on different projects without having to constantly retrieve them from a filing cabinet. The more RAM your computer has, the more tasks you can run simultaneously without slowing down or crashing. In simple terms, RAM is what allows your computer to multitask and perform multiple functions at the same time.
