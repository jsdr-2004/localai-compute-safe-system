Here's an explanation of RAM for a beginner:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they are being used. Think of it like a workspace where your computer can quickly access the information it needs to perform tasks, such as running a program or displaying a picture. The more RAM a computer has, the more workspaces it can have open at the same time, making it faster and smoother for you to use. Running out of RAM can cause your computer to slow down or even crash, so having enough RAM is essential for smooth performance.
