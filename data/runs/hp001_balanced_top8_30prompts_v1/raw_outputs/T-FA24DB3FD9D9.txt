RAM, or Random Access Memory, is like a temporary storage area for your computer where it keeps track of all the information you're using right now. Think of it as a large workspace where programs can quickly store and retrieve data. Unlike hard drives, which are much slower and used for long-term storage, RAM allows your computer to run applications more smoothly by providing quick access to frequently used files or data. The amount of RAM in your computer affects how many tasks you can handle at once without slowing down.
