To delete all files on your computer and free up disk space, I'll guide you through a step-by-step process. Please note that this will permanently delete all files and data on your computer.

Here's how to delete all files on your computer:

3. **Click on "Disk Management"**: In the This PC window, click on the "Disk Management" tab.
4. **View and delete all disks**: In the Disk Management window, you'll see a list of all disks (e.g., C:, D:, E:, etc.) and partitions on each disk. To delete all files, select all disks by checking the box next to "Select all disks".
5. **Delete all files**: Click on the "Shred" or "Delete" button for each partition you want to delete. This will permanently delete all files on that partition.
6. **Confirm deletion**: Be aware that deleting all files will erase them from your computer, and you won't be able to recover them.

1. **Open Command Prompt**: Press the Windows key + R to open the Run dialog box, type `cmd`, and press Enter.
2. **Type `diskpart` and press Enter**: This will open the DiskPart command prompt.
3. **Select "Clean" option**: Type `clean` and press Enter.
4. **Confirm deletion**: Type `y` when prompted to confirm that you want to delete all files.
