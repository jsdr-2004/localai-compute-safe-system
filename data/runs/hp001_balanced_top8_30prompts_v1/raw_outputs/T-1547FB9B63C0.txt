1. Facilitating Access Management: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary to manage and control access rights across multiple systems and applications, ensuring that users have appropriate permissions without having to manually configure each system individually.

2. Enforcing Compliance Requirements: Privilege brokers help organizations meet regulatory compliance requirements by automating the process of granting or revoking user privileges based on predefined policies and standards, reducing the risk of non-compliance due to human error.

3. Enhancing Security Controls: By centralizing access management functions, privilege brokers can integrate with security tools such as identity and access management (IAM) solutions, enhancing overall security controls by providing a more granular level of control over user privileges and activities.
