 Given that you mentioned a 16GB CPU-only laptop, it seems there might be a slight confusion as both 3B and 13B are ARM-based processor models from Mediatek, which are typically used in mobile devices rather than laptops. However, if we assume that by "CPU-only" you mean a device without a built-in graphics processing unit (GPU), then let's compare the Mediatek Helio P35 (MT6765) (3B model) and the Mediatek Helio P90 (MT6851) (13B model).

The Helio P35 is a mid-range processor with an octa-core CPU (4x2.3 GHz Cortex-A53 & 4x1.8 GHz Cortex-A53), Mali-G72 MP3 GPU, and supports up to 6GB of LPDDR4X RAM.

The Helio P90 is a higher-end processor with an octa-core CPU (2x2.2 GHz Cortex-A76 & 6x2.0 GHz Cortex-A55), Mali-G72 MP3 GPU, and supports up to 8GB of LPDDR4X RAM.

Considering a 16GB laptop scenario, the Helio P90 (13B model) would be a better choice as it offers more powerful CPU cores and is capable of handling larger amounts of RAM, although the GPU performance remains the same in both processors. However, for a CPU-only laptop, the need for a dedicated GPU might not apply, making the Helio P90 a suitable choice due to its enhanced processing capabilities.
