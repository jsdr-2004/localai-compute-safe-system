 1. A Privilege Broker acts as an intermediary between users and various system resources, managing access permissions and ensuring secure and controlled usage.

2. They are responsible for granting or denying access to system resources based on predefined policies and user privileges, maintaining the security and integrity of the system.

3. The role of a Privilege Broker often involves monitoring and auditing user activities, identifying potential security threats, and enforcing compliance with the organization's security policies.
