Here are three bullets summarizing the role of a privilege broker:

• **Facilitates negotiations and mediation**: Privilege brokers act as intermediaries between parties with conflicting interests, helping them to negotiate and mediate agreements that balance their respective needs and goals.

• **Provides expertise and guidance**: Privilege brokers often possess specialized knowledge and experience in areas such as international law, human rights, or conflict resolution, which enables them to provide valuable insights and advice to clients.

• **Resolves disputes and promotes cooperation**: By facilitating dialogue and finding mutually beneficial solutions, privilege brokers can help parties with competing interests to resolve their differences and work together towards common goals.
