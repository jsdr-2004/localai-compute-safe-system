Here's an explanation of RAM for a beginner:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer storage that temporarily holds data and applications while your computer is running. Think of it like a workspace where you can quickly access and switch between different tasks, but the information is lost when you shut down your computer. The more RAM your computer has, the more programs and files you can run at the same time without slowing down. Having enough RAM is essential for smooth performance, especially if you're running multiple resource-intensive applications simultaneously.
