Here are three bullets summarizing the role of a privilege broker:

• **Mediation and negotiation**: Privilege brokers play a key role in mediating disputes between individuals or groups with different interests, often in high-stakes situations such as corporate conflicts, intellectual property disputes, or regulatory issues.

• **Building relationships and trust**: To effectively broker privileges, brokers must establish strong relationships with all parties involved, which can involve lengthy negotiations, meetings, and communication to build trust and understanding of the other party's needs and concerns.

• **Facilitating mutually beneficial agreements**: Privilege brokers aim to create agreements that benefit all parties, often by identifying creative solutions or compromises that address multiple stakeholders' interests. This may require a deep understanding of the underlying issues, as well as effective communication and negotiation skills.
