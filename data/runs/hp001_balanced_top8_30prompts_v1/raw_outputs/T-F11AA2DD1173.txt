• **Limitations:** However, there are also some limitations associated with local AI. These include the need for substantial computational resources and storage capacity, the requirement for significant investment in hardware and infrastructure, limited scalability compared to cloud-based solutions, increased complexity and maintenance needs due to the presence of more hardware components, and potential challenges related to talent acquisition and retention.

• **Limitations:** Additionally, local AI is not suitable for all types of applications.
