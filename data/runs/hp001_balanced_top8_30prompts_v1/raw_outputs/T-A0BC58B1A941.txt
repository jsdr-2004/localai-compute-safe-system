1. Facilitating Access Management: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary to manage and control access rights for various applications and systems within an organization, ensuring that users have appropriate permissions while minimizing risks associated with unauthorized access.

2. Enforcing Compliance Standards: Privilege brokers help organizations adhere to regulatory compliance standards by enforcing strict access controls based on predefined policies and guidelines, thereby reducing the risk of non-compliance incidents.

3. Enhancing Security Posture: By centralizing and automating access management processes, privilege brokers contribute significantly to improving an organization's overall security posture. They can detect anomalies in user behavior or system activity that might indicate a security breach early on, allowing for timely intervention.
