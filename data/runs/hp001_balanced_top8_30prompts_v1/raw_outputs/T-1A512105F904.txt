1. Facilitating Access Management: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary to manage and enforce access controls across multiple systems and applications, ensuring that users have appropriate permissions for their roles.

2. Enforcing Compliance Standards: Privilege brokers help organizations meet regulatory compliance requirements by providing a centralized platform to monitor and control user privileges in real-time, thereby reducing the risk of non-compliance incidents.

3. Enhancing Security Controls: By integrating with various security tools and systems, privilege brokers can enhance overall security controls within an organization, including threat detection, incident response, and data protection measures.
