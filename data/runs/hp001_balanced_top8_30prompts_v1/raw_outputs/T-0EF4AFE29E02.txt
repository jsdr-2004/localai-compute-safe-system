1. Facilitating Access Management: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary to manage and enforce access controls across multiple systems and applications, ensuring that users have appropriate permissions for their roles.

2. Enhancing Security Compliance: By integrating with various security tools and platforms, the privilege broker helps organizations meet regulatory compliance requirements by automating and standardizing access control processes.

3. Reducing Administrative Burden: The privilege broker streamlines administrative tasks such as permission assignment and revocation, reducing the workload on IT staff while maintaining or improving system security.
