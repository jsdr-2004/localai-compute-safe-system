 A faster model can be preferable over a higher-quality model in the context of an interactive assistant for several reasons:

1. **Real-time Responses**: Interactive assistants are designed to provide instant responses to user queries. A slower, high-quality model may not be able to deliver this real-time interaction, leading to frustration for users who expect immediate feedback.

2. **User Experience**: A faster model can improve the overall user experience by reducing wait times between queries and responses. This can lead to a more enjoyable and engaging experience for users.

3. **Cost-Efficiency**: Faster models are often less complex and require fewer computational resources compared to higher-quality models. This can make them more cost-efficient in terms of both development time and resources, such as energy consumption and hardware costs.

4. **Scalability**: A faster model can be more easily scaled up to handle a larger number of users or queries without significantly degrading the performance. On the other hand, scaling up a high-quality but slow model may not provide the same level of responsiveness for all users, negatively impacting the user experience.

5. **Feedback Loops**: In an interactive setting, faster models can process more data points within a given timeframe, allowing them to learn and adapt to user preferences quicker through feedback loops. This can improve the model's performance over time.
