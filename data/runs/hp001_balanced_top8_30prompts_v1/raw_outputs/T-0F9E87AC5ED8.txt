Here's an explanation of RAM that a beginner can understand:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they're being used by the computer. It's like a workspace where your computer can quickly access and manipulate information without having to wait for it to load from a hard drive or other storage device. When you shut down your computer, all the RAM is cleared out, but when you turn it back on, it's ready to use again, allowing your computer to start up faster and run more efficiently. Think of RAM as a temporary filing system that helps your computer quickly access the information it needs to perform tasks like browsing the internet, playing games, or working with documents.
