You can use the following read-only Linux commands to inspect disk usage:

1. `df -T`: Displays detailed information about disk usage, including file system type and mount point.
2. `du -h`: Shows the size of files and directories in human-readable format (e.g., 1K, 2M, 3G).
3. `fdisk -l`: Lists all partitions on your system, along with their device names and file systems.
4. `lsblk`: Displays a list of block devices (disks) connected to your system, including partition information.
5. `cat /proc filesystems/total`: Displays the total amount of disk space available in bytes.

These commands can help you understand how much disk space is being used on your system and which partitions are consuming most of it.
