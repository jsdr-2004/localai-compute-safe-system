Here are three bullets summarizing the role of a privilege broker:

• **Advocacy for individuals**: A privilege broker acts as an advocate for individuals who have been wronged by others, such as victims of domestic abuse, human trafficking, or other forms of exploitation. They help to identify and protect vulnerable individuals from further harm.

• **Mediation and negotiation**: Privilege brokers facilitate negotiations between parties involved in disputes, with the goal of resolving conflicts and achieving mutually beneficial outcomes. This can involve mediating disputes, negotiating settlements, or facilitating communication between parties who may not otherwise be able to come to an agreement.

• **Protecting marginalized communities**: A privilege broker works to protect and support marginalized communities, including those that are disproportionately affected by systemic injustices such as racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, and ableism. They help to build coalitions, raise awareness about issues affecting these communities, and provide resources and support to individuals who have been impacted.
