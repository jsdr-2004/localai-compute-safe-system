Here's an explanation of RAM that a beginner can understand:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they're running, allowing your computer to quickly access and use the information it needs. Think of it like a desk where you can jot down notes or work on projects - just as you can easily grab a pen or piece of paper from the desk, your computer can quickly retrieve data from RAM without having to wait for something to load into its hard drive. When you shut down your computer, all the data in RAM is lost, so it's like that desk is cleared and ready for the next time you use it. The more RAM a computer has, the more applications and tasks it can run simultaneously without feeling slow or sluggish.
