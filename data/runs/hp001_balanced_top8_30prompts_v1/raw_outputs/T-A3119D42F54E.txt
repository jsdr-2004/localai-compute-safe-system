 1. A Privilege Broker acts as an intermediary, facilitating access to resources and opportunities for individuals or groups within an organization or system, based on their assigned privileges or roles.

2. They manage and enforce security policies, ensuring that only authorized users can access specific resources, thus maintaining the integrity and confidentiality of sensitive data.

3. Privilege Brokers monitor and audit user activities to identify potential threats or policy violations, helping to maintain the overall security posture of the system by detecting and mitigating unauthorized access attempts.
