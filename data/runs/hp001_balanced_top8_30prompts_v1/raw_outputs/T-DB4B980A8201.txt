For a 16GB CPU-only laptop, I would recommend a 13B (Broadcom 4300B) model instead of a 3B (Broadcom 4300B). Here's why:

1. **More memory**: A 13B model comes with 4GB of LPDDR3 RAM, which is more than enough for most users' needs, especially considering the limited storage capacity of a laptop. The additional RAM can help improve multitasking, performance, and overall system responsiveness.
2. **Better power management**: The 4300B processor has better power management features compared to the 4300A (a lower-end variant). This means it will consume less power, which is essential for battery-powered devices like laptops.
3. **Wider range of applications**: A 13B model can handle more demanding tasks, such as video editing, 3D modeling, or online gaming, without worrying about system crashes or slowdowns due to limited RAM and storage.
4. **Improved performance in multi-threaded workloads**: The 4300B processor is designed for multi-threading, which means it excels at handling multiple threads simultaneously. This makes it a better choice for users who perform tasks that involve simultaneous execution of multiple processes.

In contrast, the 3B model only offers 1GB of LPDDR2 RAM, which would be insufficient for most users' needs. Additionally, the limited power management features and lower memory capacity might lead to performance issues in demanding applications.

Overall, a 13B model provides better value and performance for users who require more RAM and better power efficiency, making it a more suitable choice for a 16GB CPU-only laptop.
