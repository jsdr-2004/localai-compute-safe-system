 1. A Privilege Broker acts as an intermediary between different entities, facilitating access to privileged information or resources that are not readily available to all users.

2. They ensure secure and controlled access, implementing authentication and authorization protocols to prevent unauthorized use of sensitive data or systems.

3. By managing permissions and settings, a Privilege Broker helps maintain security policies, enforcing principles such as least privilege and separation of duties, which are essential for maintaining system integrity and reducing potential threats.
