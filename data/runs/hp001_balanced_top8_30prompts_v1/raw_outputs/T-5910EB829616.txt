Here are three bullets summarizing the role of a privilege broker:

• **Enabling Access to Privileges**: A privilege broker acts as an intermediary between organizations or individuals seeking access to privileged information, services, or systems, and the actual providers of those privileges. They facilitate the request, negotiate terms, and ensure that access is granted securely.

• **Managing Risk and Compliance**: Privilege brokers are responsible for managing the risks associated with granting access to sensitive resources. They implement security controls, monitor usage, and enforce compliance with relevant policies and regulations to minimize the risk of data breaches or unauthorized access.

• **Providing Value-Added Services**: In addition to facilitating access and managing risk, privilege brokers often provide value-added services such as consulting on security best practices, implementing identity and access management (IAM) solutions, and delivering training and support to help organizations manage their privileged access programs.
