Here's an explanation of RAM for a beginner:

RAM, or Random Access Memory, is a type of computer storage that temporarily holds data and applications while they're being used by your computer. Think of it like a workspace where your computer can quickly access and manipulate information. The more RAM your computer has, the more programs you can run at the same time without experiencing slowdowns or crashes. When your computer needs to save the data for later use, it transfers that information from RAM to a harder drive, which is much slower but stores data longer-term.
