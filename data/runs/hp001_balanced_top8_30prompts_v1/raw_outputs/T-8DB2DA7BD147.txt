 1. A privilege broker acts as an intermediary between different entities, facilitating access to resources, opportunities, or advantages that are not generally available.

2. They negotiate and manage relationships with various authorities or systems, ensuring compliance with policies and regulations while advocating for their clients' needs and interests.

3. Their role often involves understanding the complexities of multiple systems and navigating them effectively to secure privileges such as special permissions, exemptions, or priority treatment for their clients. This can include educational institutions, government agencies, corporations, and other organizations.
