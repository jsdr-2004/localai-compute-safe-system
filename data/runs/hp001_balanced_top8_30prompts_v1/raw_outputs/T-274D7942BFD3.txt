To find out which folders are using the most disk space without deleting anything, you can follow these steps:

**For Windows:**

1. Open File Explorer (Windows key + E).
2. Click on "View" in the top menu bar.
3. Select "Show" and then check the box next to "Hidden items".
4. Right-click on "This PC" or "Computer" and select "Properties".
5. In the Properties window, click on "Disk space" under the "General" tab.
6. Click on the "View" button at the bottom of the window.
7. Select "Size" from the dropdown menu.
8. The folders with the largest size will be displayed at the top of the list.

**For macOS:**

1. Open Finder (press Command + Space to open Spotlight).
2. Press Command + Shift + 3 to take a screenshot of the disk usage.
3. Preview the screenshot and click on "Edit" > "Adjust" > "Crop".
4. Select the "Disk Usage" tab from the top menu bar.
5. Click on the "View" button at the bottom of the window.
6. Select "Size" from the dropdown menu.
7. The folders with the largest size will be displayed at the top of the list.

**For Linux:**

1. Open a terminal (e.g., Konsole, Terminal, or xterm).
2. Type `df -h` to display disk usage information.
3. Look for the folder with the highest percentage of used space.

These methods will give you an idea of which folders are using up the most disk space without deleting anything.
