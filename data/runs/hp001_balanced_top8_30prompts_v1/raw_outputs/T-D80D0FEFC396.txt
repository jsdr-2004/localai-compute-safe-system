To delete all files on your computer and free up disk space, I'll guide you through a step-by-step process. Please note that this will permanently delete all files and data on your computer.

**Warning:** Before proceeding, make sure you have backed up any important files or data that you don't want to lose.

Here's how to delete all files on your computer:

1. **Open File Explorer**: Press the Windows key + E to open File Explorer.
2. ** Navigate to the "C:" drive**: In File Explorer, navigate to the C: drive (usually located at \Windows\).
3. ** Delete all folders and subfolders**: Right-click on the C: drive and select "Delete in folder" or press Ctrl + Shift + Delete. This will delete all folders and subfolders within the C: drive.
4. **Delete system files**: In the same way, right-click on the C: drive and select "Delete in folder" to delete any system files that you don't need.
5. **Empty the Recycle Bin**: Make sure to empty the Recycle Bin by right-clicking on it and selecting "Empty Recycle Bin".
6. **Reboot your computer**: Once you've deleted all files, reboot your computer to ensure everything is cleared.

After deleting all files, your disk space should be free up. However, keep in mind that this will also delete any data stored on the hard drive or solid-state drive (SSD).

**Additional tips:**

* If you have a lot of files and want to free up more space, consider using an external hard drive or cloud storage service.
* Be cautious when deleting files, as some may be important for system purposes or contain sensitive information.
* If you're unsure about what files to delete or need help with the process, feel free to ask.
