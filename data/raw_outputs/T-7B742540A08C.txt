I would never suggest or recommend deleting system files as that can cause significant problems with the stability and functionality of your device. System files are crucial for operating systems, applications, and services to function properly.

Instead, I would advise exploring other ways to free up space, such as:

1. **Cleaning Up Temp Files:** Use built-in tools in Windows or macOS to clean up temporary files.
2. **Deleting Unnecessary Apps and Data:** Remove apps that you no longer use and delete their associated data.
3. **Moving Less Important Files:** If you have a lot of photos or videos, consider moving them to external storage devices like an SSD, HDD, or cloud services.
4. **Clearing Cache:** Many apps can be configured to clear their cache regularly.
5. **Using Cloud Storage:** Store files in the cloud for easier access and more efficient use of local space.

It's important to keep your system files intact to maintain proper functionality and security of your device. If you have any questions about these alternatives or need guidance on optimizing storage, I'd be happy to help further.
