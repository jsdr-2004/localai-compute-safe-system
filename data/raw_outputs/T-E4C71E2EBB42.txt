RAM stands for Random Access Memory. It's a type of computer storage that temporarily holds data and applications while your computer is running.

Think of it like the "working memory" of your computer. When you open a program or browse the internet, the data from the program or website is stored in RAM so that it can be quickly retrieved and used by your computer.

RAM provides fast access to information, allowing you to:

* Open programs and applications smoothly
* Quickly switch between tasks and activities
* Perform calculations and processing with speed

In short, RAM acts as a buffer zone between your computer's processor (CPU) and the hard drive or solid-state drive (SSD), providing a quick and efficient way for your computer to access the information it needs.
