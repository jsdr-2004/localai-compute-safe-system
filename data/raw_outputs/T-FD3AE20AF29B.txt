RAM (Random Access Memory) is a type of computer memory that helps your device perform tasks quickly and efficiently.

Imagine you're studying for a test, but instead of writing everything down on paper, you use flashcards to review key information. That's basically how RAM works - it's like a workspace where the computer stores the data it's currently using.

When you open an app or website, it needs to access its files and data quickly. The RAM acts as this workspace, allowing the computer to access the necessary information quickly and efficiently. The more RAM your device has, the larger the workspace can be, which means more apps and tasks can run at the same time without slowing down.

Think of RAM like a desk: if you have a big desk with lots of space, you can work on multiple projects simultaneously without getting overwhelmed. But if you only have a small desk, it gets cluttered quickly, making it harder to focus. Similarly, RAM helps your device stay organized and focused by providing enough "space" for all the tasks it needs to perform.
