RAM stands for Random Access Memory. It's a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while your computer is running.

Think of it like a desk where you can quickly grab a pen, paper, or other materials to work on. In computing, the "desk" is made up of RAM, which allows your computer to access and use the information it needs immediately.

When you open a program or browse the internet, the data from that program or website gets stored in RAM for a short time (usually a few seconds). This way, your computer can quickly retrieve the information it needs without having to wait for a longer period of time.

In simple terms, RAM is like a fast, temporary workspace where your computer can store and access data as needed.
