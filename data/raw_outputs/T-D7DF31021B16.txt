RAM (Random Access Memory) is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they're being used.

Think of it like a workspace where you can quickly access and work on things without having to go back and forth between different areas. When you open an app or file, it's stored in RAM so you can use it right away.

Here's how it works:

* Your computer uses RAM to store data that's currently being used.
* The more RAM your computer has, the more apps and files you can have open at the same time without slowing down.
* When you close an app or file, it's moved out of RAM and stored on a slower type of storage called hard drive.

In simple terms, RAM is like a temporary workspace that helps your computer work faster and more efficiently.
