RAM stands for Random Access Memory. It's a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while your computer is running.

Think of it like a workspace. Just as you can pick up pens, papers, and other materials from the workspace to work on them, RAM provides a space for your computer to store information and programs.

When you open a program or visit a website, the data (like images, text, and audio) is loaded into RAM so it can be accessed quickly. If you close the program or don't use it for a while, the data is saved back in your hard drive or other storage devices.

RAM helps your computer perform tasks faster by providing quick access to the information it needs. It's like having a shortcut to your favorite resources, so you can get things done more efficiently!
