RAM (Random Access Memory) is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they're being used.

Think of it like a workspace where you can quickly access and work on things. When you open a program or file, it's copied to the RAM so you can use it right away. The more RAM your computer has, the bigger the workspace, and the more programs you can have open at the same time without slowing down.

Here's an analogy:

* Hard drive (storage): A big filing cabinet where you store all your files and documents.
* RAM: A desk where you work on papers and projects right now. You can quickly access what you need, but when you're done, it gets put back in the filing cabinet.

When you shut down your computer, everything in the RAM is cleared out, so it's like erasing all the papers from your desk. The next time you turn it on, the workspace (RAM) is empty and ready for new projects!
