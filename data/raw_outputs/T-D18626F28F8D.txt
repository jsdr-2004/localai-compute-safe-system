RAM (Random Access Memory) is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while your computer is running.

Think of it like a workspace where you can work on a project, but once you're done, you need to clean up and put things back in their original place. RAM does the same thing for your computer - it "works" on the information you give it, and then gets rid of it when you're done.

Here's how it works:

1. Your computer asks your hard drive (a slower storage device) where it can find some data.
2. The hard drive says, "Oh, I have that data right here!" and sends it to RAM.
3. RAM uses the data while your computer is running on it (like a workspace).
4. When you're done with the task, RAM gets rid of the data.

The more RAM your computer has, the bigger the workspace, and the more projects it can work on at the same time without slowing down. That's why having enough RAM is important for smooth performance!
