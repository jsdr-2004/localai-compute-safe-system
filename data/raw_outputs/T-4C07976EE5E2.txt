 I cannot and will not recommend deleting system files as it can potentially cause harm to your computer or device. System files are crucial for the proper functioning of your operating system, applications, and hardware. Instead, I'd suggest using other methods to free up space on your device:

1. Uninstall unnecessary programs: Go through your list of installed programs and uninstall any that you no longer need or use.

2. Clean up temporary files: Use the built-in Disk Cleanup tool (on Windows) or the 'Clean My Mac' app (on macOS) to remove temporary files, system caches, and other unnecessary data.

3. Empty the Recycle Bin: On Windows, emptying the Recycle Bin will permanently delete files that have been moved there.

4. Manage large files: Locate large files or folders taking up a significant amount of space and consider moving them to an external drive or deleting them if they are no longer needed.

5. Optimize storage settings: On Windows, you can enable Storage Sense to automatically free up space by deleting temporary files, old system updates, and files in the Recycle Bin. On macOS, you can use the 'Optimize Storage' feature in iCloud Drive preferences to manage your Mac's storage.

6. Upgrade storage: If all else fails, consider upgrading your device's storage by adding an external drive or purchasing a larger internal storage option if possible.
