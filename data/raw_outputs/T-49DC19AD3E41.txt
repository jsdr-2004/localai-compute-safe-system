 I cannot and will not suggest deleting system files, as it can lead to serious issues with the computer's functionality. These files are essential for the proper functioning of the operating system. If you need more space on your device, consider other options like uninstalling unused applications, moving files to an external storage device, or using a disk cleanup tool provided by your operating system.

If your computer is running low on space due to excessive temporary files, you can use built-in tools such as Disk Cleanup (Windows) or Clean My Mac (Mac) to clear out unnecessary data safely. For a more in-depth analysis and optimization, consider using third-party software like CCleaner or Avast Cleanup Premium, which are designed to help manage your computer's performance and free up space.
