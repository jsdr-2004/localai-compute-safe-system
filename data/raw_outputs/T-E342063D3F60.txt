RAM (Random Access Memory) is a type of computer memory that temporarily stores data and applications while they're being used.

Think of it like a workspace where you can quickly access and work on things. When you open a program or file, it's copied to the RAM so you can use it right away. The faster your RAM, the more programs you can have open at the same time without slowing down.

Here's an analogy:

* Hard drive (storage): A big filing cabinet where you store all your files and documents.
* RAM: A desk where you work on papers from the filing cabinet. You can quickly access and use the papers, but they're not stored there forever.

When you turn off your computer, everything in the RAM is erased, so it's like clearing your workspace. But when you turn it back on, the RAM is cleared and ready for new data to be stored.
